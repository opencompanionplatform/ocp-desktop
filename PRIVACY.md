# Open Companion Platform Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 13 September 2026**

Open Companion Platform (OCP) is an open-source desktop companion platform. This policy explains what information OCP may process when you use the desktop application, OCP Store, account features, cloud services, and optional third-party integrations.

## Privacy principles

- OCP is designed to keep desktop functionality local where practical.
- OCP does **not sell personal information**.
- Network access occurs when you use a feature that requires a network service, such as sign-in, the Store, cloud synchronization, software updates, purchases, or an optional AI/voice provider.
- Optional providers are used only when the relevant feature is enabled or requested by the user.
- OCP does not intentionally use private chat or voice content as general advertising data.

## Information OCP may process

The exact information depends on the features you use.

### Account and authentication information

When you create or use an OCP account, OCP may process:

- email address and authentication identifiers;
- account/profile identifiers;
- sign-in/session information required to keep the account authenticated;
- account security and device-registration information needed to protect access.

Authentication credentials are handled by the configured authentication service. OCP should not store your plaintext account password.

### Store, library, progression, and purchase information

When you use online Store or cloud features, OCP may process information such as:

- characters or other content associated with your account;
- library and entitlement records;
- installation/download status;
- progression or account-state records when cloud progression is enabled;
- order status, product identifiers, prices, and transaction references needed to provide purchases.

Payment-card information is handled by the payment provider. OCP does not need to receive or store full payment-card numbers in order to maintain purchase entitlements.

### Community and support content

If you submit a review, rating, report, creator response, support request, or other user-generated content, OCP processes the content and account information required to provide and moderate that feature.

Do not include passwords, secret keys, private authentication tokens, or unnecessary sensitive personal information in reviews or support messages.

### Runtime, device, and update information

OCP may process limited technical information when required for features such as device registration, update delivery, security, or troubleshooting. This can include application version, platform/architecture, a device or installation identifier, request timestamps, and technical error information.

OCP is not intended to collect unrelated files, browsing history, or keystrokes as general telemetry.

### AI and voice features

AI and voice features are optional.

If you choose a cloud AI or voice provider, the text, prompt, or other content necessary to perform the requested feature may be transmitted to that provider. The provider's own privacy terms apply to its processing.

When OCP is configured to use a local provider such as a locally hosted model, processing may remain on the user's device or local network, depending on the user's configuration.

## Third-party services

OCP may rely on third-party services for specific online features. Current or planned service categories include:

- **Supabase** — authentication, account data, database/API functions, and related cloud services;
- **Cloudflare** — website delivery, CDN/object storage, and related network infrastructure;
- **Stripe** — payment processing when purchases are enabled;
- **Google Gemini or another provider selected/configured by the user** — optional cloud AI and/or voice processing.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. OCP may add, remove, or change providers as the project evolves; material changes to OCP's own data practices should be reflected in this policy.

## Local data

The desktop application stores settings and runtime data locally so OCP can remember user preferences and installed companion content. Removing the application may not automatically remove all user-data directories, because retaining settings can support upgrades and reinstalls.

Users who want a complete local reset should remove OCP user-data directories after exporting anything they want to keep.

## Data sharing

OCP may share information with service providers only as needed to operate a feature requested by the user, process a purchase, deliver content, maintain security, comply with law, or protect the project and its users.

OCP does not sell personal information to advertisers.

## Data retention and deletion

Cloud data is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant account, entitlement, security, support, or legal function.

Users may request deletion of account-associated data that OCP controls, subject to records that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, transaction, or legal obligations.

## Security

OCP uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect project and user data, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Please report security vulnerabilities using the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through public issues.

## Children's privacy

OCP is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If the project learns that personal information was collected in circumstances requiring deletion under applicable law, the maintainers will take reasonable steps to address it.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when OCP features or service providers change. Material changes should be published in the public source repository and on the public privacy-policy page.
